About CertiTrust

Verify Every Credential. Trust Every Certificate.

CertiTrust helps institutions issue tamper-proof digital certificates and lets anyone check them in seconds.

Our mission

Fake certificates cost employers time and honest graduates their credibility. CertiTrust gives every certificate a unique ID, a QR code and a cryptographic fingerprint, so authenticity can be proven instantly instead of by e-mail and phone calls.

How the protection works

When a certificate is issued, a SHA-256 hash of its key data is stored. On every verification the hash is recomputed. If anybody changes the record afterwards, the two values differ and the certificate is flagged as tampered.

Who uses it

Issuers create and revoke certificates, verifiers (recruiters, employers) check them and keep a history, and administrators manage users, institutions and audit logs. Anyone can verify a certificate without an account.

Technology

Java 17, Spring Boot, Spring Security, Spring Data JPA, MySQL, Thymeleaf, Bootstrap 5, ZXing (QR codes) and OpenPDF (PDF certificates).
